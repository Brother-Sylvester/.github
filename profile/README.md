# Brother Sylvester

**Afrocentric education for children and families: African and Caribbean history, taught with love and sensitivity.**

[brothersylvester.com](https://www.brothersylvester.com)

Sylvester Leader was born in the Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis, on Saint Kitts, one of ten children and a twin alongside his brother. He has 45+ years in education, including teaching, careers guidance and policy planning, in the UK, Uganda, Kenya, and Ghana, and the Caribbean.

The website holds his essays, a history library and resources for parents, and takes bookings from schools and partners. The Sister Marilyn Harella Wilkes Leader Foundation funds scholarships and teacher training in Uganda, Kenya, and Ghana.

The website's code is private.
